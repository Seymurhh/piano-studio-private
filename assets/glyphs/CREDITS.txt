Glyph outlines used by assets/js/glyphs.js for the 3D lettering of the sheet-music view

Both files in this folder are converted subsets of open fonts. They stay under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (full text and copyright notices in OFL.txt), not under the licence of this app. They may
be bundled with the app and sold as part of it, but not sold on their own. They are Modified Versions
in the licence's sense, so they are not offered under the fonts' Reserved Font Names: they are
described below as outlines "converted from" each font.


music.json - music symbols
--------------------------
Converted from: Bravura, version 1.482, the reference font of the Standard Music Font Layout (SMuFL)
Designer:       Daniel Spreadbury et al.
Copyright:      Copyright © 2026, Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH, with Reserved Font Name "Bravura".
                Bravura is a registered trademark of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.
Licence:        SIL Open Font License 1.1
Source:         https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura
                commit 37b194378b710cc40e406ab6c4b07608bb9548ae (24 August 2026):
                redist/otf/Bravura.otf, redist/Bravura.json (metadata), LICENSE.txt
                Symbol names and code points: SMuFL glyphnames.json,
                https://github.com/w3c-cg/smufl (metadata/glyphnames.json)
What was changed (converted subset):
  - 51 symbols taken by SMuFL code point: clefs (G, F, C), brace, accidentals, rests, flags,
    noteheads, augmentation dot, time-signature digits and common/cut time, metronome notes, repeat
    dot, segno, coda, fermata, dynamics p f m, trill, tuplet 3 and 6.
  - Outlines rewritten as SVG-like path strings in staff spaces (font units divided by 250, since
    1 em = 4 staff spaces), y up, origin at each symbol's SMuFL origin, rounded to 1/1000 of a
    staff space. Contours were re-wound (filled parts counter-clockwise, holes clockwise) and
    zero-length segments removed; the shapes were checked against the original font's fill.
  - Advance widths and bounding boxes recomputed from the outlines (they match Bravura.json);
    glyph anchors (for example stemUpSE, stemDownNW on noteheads, stemUpNW, stemDownSW on flags)
    and the engraving defaults (staff line, stem, beam and ledger line thicknesses) copied from
    Bravura.json.
  - Everything else left out: all other glyphs, hinting, OpenType tables and features.


text.json - title and tempo text
--------------------------------
Converted from: Libre Baskerville Regular, version 2.005
Designers:      Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida
Copyright:      Copyright (c) 2012, Pablo Impallari; Copyright (c) 2012, Rodrigo Fuenzalida;
                with Reserved Font Name Libre Baskerville (the notice in the upstream OFL.txt,
                reproduced in OFL.txt here). The font file itself names the holders as
                "The Libre Baskerville Project Authors
                (https://github.com/impallari/Libre-Baskerville)".
                Libre Baskerville is a trademark of Pablo Impallari.
Licence:        SIL Open Font License 1.1
Source:         https://github.com/impallari/Libre-Baskerville
                commit 9852edf75ece3af500a5ec61245f94788c3d4633 (16 October 2025):
                fonts/ttf/LibreBaskerville-Regular.ttf, OFL.txt
                (in that OFL.txt the "@" of the second e-mail address is a stray byte; it is
                written as "@" in OFL.txt here, otherwise the notice is unchanged)
What was changed (converted subset):
  - 330 characters: printable ASCII, Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A (for titles such as
    Gymnopédie, Träumerei, Für Elise, Dvořák), Ș ș Ț ț, en and em dashes, curly quotes, bullet,
    ellipsis. The font has no soft hyphen, ŉ or ſ, so those are not included.
  - Outlines rewritten as SVG-like path strings in ems (font units divided by 1000), y up,
    baseline at y = 0, rounded to 1/1000 em; contours re-wound and zero-length segments removed
    as above; advance widths and bounding boxes kept; ascender, descender, cap height and x-height
    copied.
  - Kerning: pair values read from the font's GPOS table ('kern' feature, Latin script, the values
    of all its pair-adjustment lookups added together, as a text shaper applies them), in
    thousandths of an em, stored as kern[left][right]: all 3,769 pairs of at least 4/1000 em
    between unaccented characters (spaces included), plus 246 pairs with an accented letter where
    the font's value differs by 4/1000 em or more from the pair of the base letters. Any other
    accented pair uses the pair of its base letters.
  - Everything else left out: all other glyphs, hinting, other OpenType features.


Conversion
----------
Made with opentype.js 1.3.4 (MIT licence; used only for the conversion, not shipped with the app).
