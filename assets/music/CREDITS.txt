Pieces in the studio's library

All the music is in the public domain (the composers died between 1750 and 1943).
The MIDI files were made from the free editions of the Mutopia Project (https://www.mutopiaproject.org; the two Passacaglias from IMSLP)
with LilyPond 2.24; each edition's own licence is given below and still applies to the file made from it.

handel-passacaglia-g-minor.smf
  George Frideric Handel: Passacaglia (Passacaille) in G minor, from the Suite No. 7, HWV 432 (London, 1720)
  Notes entered by hand after the edition typeset by Pierre Gouin (Les Éditions Outremontaises, 2006; Werner Icking
  Music Collection; after the Hallische Händel-Ausgabe), IMSLP #130582, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This MIDI file is shared under the same licence. Changes: re-entered
  as MIDI with LilyPond 2.24, ornaments played out, the theme played twice as in the edition, and the small note in bar 41
  marked "only on the repeat" left out; then given a performance without moving any note: dynamics (building through the
  variations, with the melody and the ground bass voiced over the inner parts), a tempo for each variation from 62 (the
  theme) to 70 with a short breath at the end of each and a broadening to 42 on the last chord, and a legato sustain pedal
  on the half-bars that hold one chord and on the last chord. Bar 24's falling triplets are on the bass staff, as the
  edition prints them.

handel-halvorsen-passacaglia.smf
  Handel / Halvorsen: Passacaglia, piano arrangement by Lumen Keys (2026)
  Arranged from Johan Halvorsen's Passacaglia for violin and viola (1894), itself after the Passacaille of Handel's
  Suite No. 7 in G minor, HWV 432 (1720). Source: the first edition (Wilhelm Hansen, Copenhagen, n.d. [1896], plate
  11808), IMSLP #72041, public domain. Every note of the 54 bars used was read twice, independently, from the scan and
  the two readings compared note by note. The arrangement (the choice of variations, the piano writing, Handel's ground
  bass added under the theme and the first variation, and the performance: tempo, dynamics, voicing and pedal) is
  Lumen Keys' own.

Changes made here: a tempo was set where the edition's file had none or an unsuitable one (Rondo alla Turca 126,
Prelude in C 70, Moonlight Sonata first movement 54 and third movement 160, Gymnopédie 66, Arabesque 104). The sustain
pedal was added by a simple rule (change the pedal when the bass note changes) to: Prelude in C, Moonlight Sonata first
movement, Nocturne, Clair de lune, Gymnopédie, Träumerei, Arabesque, Pathétique second movement, Revolutionary Étude,
Wedding Day at Troldhaugen, Prelude in G minor, Fantasia in D minor. The Gnossienne and the two Passacaglias were entered
by hand from their editions (the Passacaglias' from IMSLP, not the Mutopia Project); the Passacaglias' dynamics, tempo
and pedal were shaped by hand (see above), not by the simple rule.

The files end in .smf (Standard MIDI File) instead of .mid: they are ordinary MIDI files, renamed because this site
never publishes .mid files, so that her own recordings cannot be published by accident.
