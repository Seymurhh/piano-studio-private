Sounds used by the piano studio

grand.pack
  Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm: recordings of a Yamaha C5 grand piano.
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 (MP3 edition from the npm packages
  @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity4 and -velocity12).
  Changes made here: two of the sixteen loudness layers are used, each recording is shortened to
  at most 9 seconds with a fade, and re-encoded as MP3.
  "Bright Grand", "Soft Felt" and "Honky-tonk Upright" are made from the same recordings by
  filtering, blending and detuning them in the browser.

electric_grand_piano.pack, electric_piano_1.pack, harpsichord.pack
  FluidR3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen (MIT licence), as MP3 notes from the npm package
  soundfont-for-samplers. Every third note is included; the notes between are played by
  shifting the nearest one.

File format of a .pack: 4 bytes (length of the index, little-endian), a JSON index
{files:[{p: MIDI pitch, l: loudness layer, o: offset, n: bytes}]}, then the MP3 files end to end.
